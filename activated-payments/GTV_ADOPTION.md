# Payments GTV Adoption — methodology

**Where it lives:** Facadash → Payments → Funnel, at the foot of the page.
**Built by:** `sql/gtv_adoption_monthly.sql` → `pull.js` (`pullAdoption`) → `gtv-adoption.json` → a
`window.__GTV_ADOPTION` block appended to `funnel-data.js` → the section in `index.html`.
**Tests:** `node activated-payments/test/gtv_adoption.test.js` (20 checks).

## The question

> Of all POS sales volume generated by credible US Loyverse merchants in a month, what share
> belongs to merchants who had already taken at least one successful Loyverse Payments
> transaction by the end of that month?

## What it is NOT

**This is merchant adoption weighted by till volume. It is not payments penetration.** The
numerator is an adopting merchant's *entire* POS figure, including every sale they still take in
cash. A merchant who runs one card payment through Loyverse Payments and rings everything else
through cash counts in full.

Read as penetration it overstates the business by a wide margin. The section's own subtitle and
the note under the table both say so; do not quote the number without that sentence attached.

## Definitions

| | |
|---|---|
| **Numerator** | POS GTV of credible merchants whose first succeeded + paid + captured Loyverse Payments charge fell on or before the month end |
| **Denominator** | POS GTV of every credible merchant in the same calendar month |
| **Credible** | `GTV_CONFIDENCE` of `1 - Confirmed by card data` or `2 - Plausible` |
| **POS GTV** | `LOYVERSE_RECEIPTS`, USD only, `TOTAL_MONEY > 0`, not cancelled, not a refund, `RECEIPT_TYPE <> 'REFUND'`, each receipt capped at `$10,000 × GTV_SCALE` |
| **Adoption** | first successful charge, from `CONNECTED_ACCOUNT_CHARGES` where `status = 'succeeded' AND paid AND captured AND currency = 'USD'` |
| **Grain** | one calendar month, one row; a merchant counts once per month |
| **From** | July 2026, the US launch |

Every definition is inherited from `merchant-base-automation/queries/q1_base.sql`, so this section
and the US Merchant Base agree by construction rather than by coincidence.

## The three decisions worth knowing

**1. The credibility tier is recomputed monthly, not carried backwards.**
`GTV_CONFIDENCE` in the merchant base is computed on a trailing-30-day window at snapshot time.
Applying today's tier to July would substitute a current judgement for a historical one, and the
brief requires that to be labelled an estimate. Instead the same `CASE` is re-evaluated on each
month's own volume, median ticket and accumulated card history, so **July is judged on July**.
That is what makes these months *observed*.

**2. Merchants are joined on `owner_id`, not email.**
`q1_base.sql` matches Stripe accounts to merchants on email and business name — measured at 97.5%
of 684 US accounts — and its header records that the Stripe share "carries NO Loyverse identifier".
That is no longer true. `CONNECTED_ACCOUNTS_METADATA` carries `owner_id`, verified 2026-11-19 at
**1,943 of 1,945 accounts (99.9%)** resolving to a merchant. This section uses `owner_id` alone.
The email/name fallback is deliberately *not* carried over: it left four name-shared accounts
unlinked on purpose, because picking one merchant from a generic business name is a coin flip.

**3. Completed months are frozen.**
A closed month is written once and never rewritten. The open month refreshes on every run and is
labelled **month to date** — drawn dashed, excluded from the month-over-month column, and never
described as a monthly figure, because adopters and the base accumulate at different rates within
a month and the ratio is not stable until the month closes. To force a recalculation after a data
correction, delete that month's entry from `gtv-adoption.json`; the next run rebuilds it.

### Against the provisional estimates

| Month | measured | provisional |
|---|---:|---:|
| Jul | 1.30% | 0.95% |
| Aug | 2.10% | 1.88% |
| Sep | 3.21% | 2.93% |
| Oct | 3.04% | 3.10% |

The provisional July–September numerators were reconstructed from **October** trailing-30-day
volume grouped by first-charge date, and held the denominator constant at $49.25m. Measured on
each month's own receipts the numerators are higher and the denominators move: September is
$48.50m, October $58.23m.

### October against the 30 October merchant base

| | measured (calendar Oct) | snapshot (trailing 30d to 30 Oct) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Credible merchants | 4,094 | ~3,530 |
| Total POS GTV | $58.23m | $51.24m |
| Adoption | 3.04% | 3.10% |

Two windows and two rules. The snapshot is trailing-30-days and classifies each merchant on that
one window; this is calendar October and reclassifies every merchant on October alone. **The
merchant count is where the gap sits** (+16%), and the GTV follows it (+13.6%). The adoption
*ratio* barely moves, because both sides grow together — which is the reassuring part.

Daily run-rate as a sanity check: Sep $1.62m/day, Oct $1.88m/day, Nov $1.88m/day. October's higher
total is a real step up in run-rate, not a window artefact.

## Limitations

- **US only.** The credibility rule is US- and USD-only by construction. Other markets need a
  credibility definition that does not exist yet; the per-currency outlier bound used by the
  Facadash country table is a candidate but is a *different* rule and gives a US denominator
  around $201m rather than $49m. The two must not be mixed in one chart.
- **Refunds and cancellations** are excluded at the receipt level, not netted off. A sale refunded
  in a later month stays in the earlier month's GTV.
- **Currency conversion** does not arise: the denominator is USD receipts only.
- **The $10k per-receipt cap** is the merchant base's, kept so the two agree. It is a blunter
  instrument than the per-currency bound used elsewhere on Facadash.
- **A merchant that stops trading** leaves both numerator and denominator, so adoption is a
  share of *active* volume, not of all merchants ever acquired.
