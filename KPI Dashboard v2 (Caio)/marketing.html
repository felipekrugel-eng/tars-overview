Spend & traffic

Cost vs conversions

Daily spend (bars, left axis) against conversions (line, right axis). Divergence between the two is the story — flat bars with a falling line means efficiency is decaying.

Clicks vs impressions

Reach (impressions, right axis) against engagement (clicks, left axis). Impressions climbing while clicks stay flat means the auction is serving weaker inventory.
Conversion events

Blended cost per event

Total spend in the period divided by each event’s volume. This is a blended figure, not attributed cost — Google Ads does not split spend across events, so read it as “what each event cost us on average”, not as its true marginal cost.

Event volume trend

The five highest-volume events over time. Cumulative smooths out daily noise; daily shows the actual shape.
Funnel

Where volume falls away

Every conversion event ranked by volume, with the step-over-step drop to the next one. Ordering is by volume, not by a fixed funnel definition — events are not guaranteed to be sequential stages.
Detail

Event performance

Volume and blended cost for every conversion event in the filtered period.

Campaign settings

Current state of each campaign as of the last export — status, bid strategy and daily budget.
