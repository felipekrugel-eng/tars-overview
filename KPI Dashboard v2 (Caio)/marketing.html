POS path

Did the till ever ring. This is activation for the core product and it does not require Payments.

Payments path

Opening a Stripe account, getting through KYC, and taking a first card payment. Each step is a subset of the one above it.
These two paths are not steps in one ladder. A merchant can ring up a sale, open Payments, do both or do neither — so there is no conversion rate between them and none is shown. Percentages inside each path are of the step above; percentages beside the bars are of everyone who signed up.

Spend and merchants, by day

Bars are spend on the chosen campaigns. The line counts merchants on the date basis selected above, so a merchant lands on the day we bought their click, not the day they got round to signing up.

Cost per merchant, rolling 7 days

Daily cost per merchant is meaningless at this volume — a day with one signup reads as a triumph or a disaster. This is spend over the window divided by merchants in the window, not an average of daily ratios.
